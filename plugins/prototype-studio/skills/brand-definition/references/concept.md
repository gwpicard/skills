# The core idea

Assembling a brand bottom-up (palette, then type, then a moodboard, then a screen) produces safe parts that add up to a generic whole. A brand needs one core idea that the parts express.

## Where good ideas come from

- **The decision-maker's own sketch.** A rough drawing of how they explain their work often beats many agent concepts. Ask for it early: "How do you draw this on a whiteboard?"
- **The customer's real world.** A real office, real software screens, real materials, real routines. Metaphors drawn from the customer's operations land; metaphors drawn from the vendor's offer do not.
- **The organisation's point of view.** A scale, a stance or a decision rule can be shown as well as said.

For a firm whose product is judgement or expertise, consider showing the judgement working, for example an interactive tool that applies the method to the visitor's situation, rather than describing it. An architecture firm shows buildings; an AI firm can show its thinking at work.

## Ideas that fail

- **A diagram of the offer.** Boxes for the three stages, arrows between them. Variations on it feel like "thinking inside the box".
- **Literal props from the wrong world.** Machines, trucks, megaphones, school paper.
- **Abstract symbols.** Upward graphs, targets, light bulbs, brains.
- **Code-first concepts.** Ideas shaped by what is easy to build in the chosen library. Sketch or generate images first, then decide how to build.
- **Prototypes that vary the mechanism, not the meaning.** Six concepts that are six animations of the same diagram are one concept.

## Test the idea

- Can the user say it in one sentence?
- Could a competitor use it unchanged? If so, it belongs to nobody.
- Does it survive at the smallest size and the shortest attention span: a social image, a favicon, the first screen?

## Storyboard before building

For any idea that unfolds over scroll or time, storyboard it as a sequence of frames (image-generated or sketched) and agree the beats before writing code. Check pacing in the storyboard: a story that takes twenty screens before the offer appears belongs on its own page.

Settle the asset style (stage 5) before polishing the story. A story polished for days in a style that is later rejected must be redone.
